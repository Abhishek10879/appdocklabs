Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

Part 107 Drone Test Prep 2027 has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your study progress stays on your phone. We never sell your data and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

The app is made by AppDock Labs, an independent developer based in India, which is the data controller for anything you send us. Questions about your data: appdocklabs01@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your answers, scores, mock exams, review list, exam date and reminder settings are saved only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. Settings → Reset progress clears them at any time.

Purchases

Purchases are made and processed entirely by Apple. The app asks Apple on your phone whether this Apple ID owns Pro; nothing is sent to us or to any other company. We never see your name, email or card details.

Apple keeps purchase records for billing and tax under Apple’s own privacy policy. To cancel or request a refund, use your App Store account settings or reportaproblem.apple.com.

We don’t share any data with third parties, and there are no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or tracking in the app.

Problem reports

If you report a problem with a question, your email app sends us the question number, the app version and whatever you write. We use it only to fix the question and reply to you (our legitimate interest in keeping the content accurate), and delete it within 12 months.

Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent from our servers, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Children

The app is for people preparing for an FAA test (minimum age 16). It is not aimed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Wherever you live (including under UK/EU GDPR, U.S. state privacy laws and India’s DPDP Act) you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because almost everything stays on your phone, we normally hold nothing about you at all, apart from any email you send us, which we delete on request. We answer within 30 days. UK users can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk); EU users to their local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for any important change, tell you in the app.
