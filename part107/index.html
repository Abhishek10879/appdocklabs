Support

Questions, a wrong answer you spotted, or trouble with a purchase? Email appdocklabs01@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Report a problem with a question

Open the question, tap Report a problem with this question under the explanation, and say what looks wrong. The question number is filled in for you. Please don’t send questions from your actual FAA test: test content is confidential.

Restore a purchase

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with. Nothing else is needed; there is no account.

Cancel the weekly plan or ask for a refund

Subscriptions and refunds are handled by Apple: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

About the app
